     to switch.

B. INVITING CAREGIVERS  (Settings > Family & caregivers; needs internet)
   - The owner taps "Invite a caregiver", gets a 6-letter code and shares
     it (WhatsApp etc.). The code works once and expires in 7 days.
   - The other person installs the app, signs up, and on the first screen
     taps "Joining a family? Enter the invite code".
   - Owners can remove caregivers; caregivers can leave.
   - Database: new migration 20260929000004_family_invites.sql (already
     applied to the cloud project).
   TEST: needs a second account (e.g. another email). Invite it, join, and
   check the baby and entries appear on the second phone or account.

C. "AT A GLANCE" ON THE TODAY TAB
   Shown only when there's enough data:
   - Hunger guide: "Usually feeds about every 2h 50m, so the next feed may
     be around 1:50 PM", from this baby's last 3 days (median gap; night
     gaps over 8 h are ignored). Needs about 6+ feeds logged.
   - Sleep: "Usually sleeps about 13h a day. Today so far: 10h 45m" (median
     of the last 7 days that have 8h+ logged; needs 3 such days).
   - "Awake for 1h 20m (usually about 2h between sleeps)".
   - Overdue or next vaccines (within 30 days), today's medicine doses
     recorded, and latest weight. Tapping one opens that tab.

D. STARTING SOLIDS  (new "Solids" tab, 3rd at the bottom)
   - Guides: "Is my baby ready?", how textures change (6 / 7-9 / 10-12 m),
     important rules (no salt/sugar/honey, no whole nuts, quarter grapes,
     stay with baby), signs of an allergic reaction (with 1122 / 115 for
     emergencies), and "Common beliefs vs evidence" (ghutti with honey,
     water in heat under 6 months, chai, cereal for sleep, delaying
     allergens), as the plan asked.
   - Allergen tracker: egg, milk, peanut, nuts, sesame, wheat, fish, each
     shown as Not yet / Introduced / Reaction.
   - 36 foods, including Pakistani staples (khichdi, daliya, suji, moong and
     masoor daal, lauki, chikoo, qeema, dahi...), with search and category
     filters. Tap one for how to prepare it, allergen and choking notes,
     "Log a try" (date, liked/okay/disliked, reaction none/mild/severe,
     notes), and the history. A severe reaction shows "call 1122/115 now".
   - Sources: NHS weaning pages, WHO. The content is in
     app/tool/content/make_foods.py and needs pediatrician review (item 23).

E. MILESTONES  (Growth tab > "Milestones")
   - CDC 2022 checklists for 2, 4, 6, 9, 12, 15 and 18 months (86 items),
     grouped by social, language, learning and movement. The baby's current
