   Shown only when there's enough data:
   - Hunger guide: "Usually feeds about every 2h 50m, so the next feed may
     be around 1:50 PM", from this baby's last 3 days (median gap; night
     gaps over 8 h are ignored). Needs about 6+ feeds logged.
   - Sleep: "Usually sleeps about 13h a day. Today so far: 10h 45m" (median
     of the last 7 days that have 8h+ logged; needs 3 such days).
   - "Awake for 1h 20m (usually about 2h between sleeps)".
   - Overdue or next vaccines (within 30 days), today's medicine doses
     recorded, and latest weight. Tapping one opens that tab.

   - Guides: "Is my baby ready?", how textures change (6 / 7-9 / 10-12 m),

     water in heat under 6 months, chai, cereal for sleep, delaying
     allergens), as the plan asked.

   - 36 foods, including Pakistani staples (khichdi, daliya, suji, moong and
