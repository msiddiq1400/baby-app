Switch from Huckleberry

Bring your Huckleberry log to Palna.

Huckleberry's free tracking may be all you need. If you'd like the whole family on one log, your country's vaccine schedule, or the app in Urdu, you can move your baby's history into Palna in a few minutes.

Three steps

How to move your log

  1. Export from Huckleberry

    Tap your child's icon in the menu bar, scroll down and tap Export tracking data as CSV. Huckleberry emails you a link that works for 24 hours. Open it and download the CSV.

  2. Open Palna

    Open Settings, then Your data > Export and import, and pick Huckleberry.

  3. Import

    Tap Choose file and pick the CSV. Check the preview, then tap Import. Entries already in Palna aren't doubled.

Tip: don't open the file in Excel and save it before importing, because Excel changes the times. If that has happened, export the file again.

What comes across

Your history, not a fresh start.

Imported

  • Feeds
  • Sleep
  • Diapers
  • Pumping
  • Growth measurements
  • Temperatures
  • Activities

Not imported

  • Medicines
  • Solid foods
  • Vaccines
  • Milestones

These are listed in the preview, so you can see what was left out. You can enter them again in Palna's Health section.

Questions

Frequently asked

Is Palna free?

Yes. Everything in the Free column above is free, including the import, and there are no ads. Palna Pro is optional: it adds more than one baby, next nap and bedtime times, 30-day reports, the PDF summary for the doctor, more photos and colour themes. One Pro plan covers the whole family: monthly, yearly or lifetime.

Will my partner see the imported log?

Yes. Imported entries are added to your baby's log, so everyone you invite to the family sees them on their own phone. To invite someone, open Settings, then Family & caregivers > Invite a caregiver. See how family sharing works.

What about twins?

Export a file for each child from Huckleberry, and import each one into its own baby in Palna: choose the baby first, then its file. The Free plan covers one baby; for a second baby you'll need Palna Pro.

What if I import the same file twice?

Nothing is doubled. Entries already in Palna are skipped, so you can import a newer export later to bring in the days since.

The email link from Huckleberry has expired.

The link works for 24 hours. Export again from Huckleberry to get a new one.
