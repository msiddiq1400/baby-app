Pakistan's EPI vaccine schedule, by age

Updated 28 October 2026

Pakistan's Expanded Programme on Immunization (EPI) gives babies their routine vaccines free of charge at government vaccination centres. Six visits between birth and 15 months protect your child against 12 serious diseases. Here is the schedule, visit by visit, as published by the Federal Directorate of Immunization.

The schedule

Source: Federal Directorate of Immunization (EPI Pakistan), routine schedule. 19 doses in all.
VisitAgeVaccines
1At birthBCG · Oral polio (OPV-0) · Hepatitis B (birth dose)

615 monthsMeasles-rubella (MR) 2

Oral polio and rotavirus are drops given by mouth; the others are injections.

Good to know

  • Take the vaccination card to every visit, so each dose is written down.
  • If a visit was missed, don't wait for the next one. Go to your vaccination centre and ask the vaccinator how to catch up.
  • Follow your vaccinator. Schedules are updated from time to time, and provinces can differ, so always go by the dates your vaccinator or child specialist gives you.
  • Extra vaccines. Private clinics may offer vaccines that aren't part of EPI. Ask your child specialist whether any are right for your baby.
  • Questions? The EPI schedule poster gives the government's Sehat Tahaffuz helpline, 1166, for more information.
