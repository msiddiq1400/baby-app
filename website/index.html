Most baby apps assume a US vaccine schedule, American food and a steady connection. Palna starts from how babies are raised in Pakistan.

Questions

Frequently asked

When can I download Palna?

Palna is in final testing and coming to Google Play and the App Store soon. Email hello@palnacare.com and we'll tell you the day it launches.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Everything you log is saved on your phone first, so Palna works in load-shedding, on a weak signal or with no data at all. It syncs with your family when you're back online.

Can the whole family use it?

Yes. The parent who adds the baby can invite others with a 6-letter code (it works once and expires in 7 days). Everyone sees the same baby, and each person can choose English, Urdu or Roman Urdu.

Twins or more than one child?

Add as many babies as you like and switch between them from the home screen.

Is the vaccine schedule official?

It follows Pakistan's Expanded Programme on Immunization (EPI). Schedules change and can differ by province, so always confirm dates with your vaccinator or child specialist.

Is Palna medical advice?

No. Palna shows general guidance from the WHO, NHS, CDC and Pakistan's EPI and helps you keep records. It doesn't diagnose anything. If you're worried, contact a doctor; in an emergency call Rescue 1122 or Edhi 115.

Who can see my baby's information?

Only you and the family members you invite. We don't sell data or show ads. See the privacy policy.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Settings, then Delete my account. Or email us. Details are on the delete your account page.
