# Invite message for Android testers

Send on WhatsApp once the closed test is approved. Replace `<LINK>` with the
opt-in link from Play Console > Test and release > Closed testing > Testers
("Join on Android" / "Join on the web"). Google needs at least 12 testers who
stay opted in for 14 days in a row, so invite 15–20 people.

---

## English

Assalam o alaikum! I've made **Palna**, a baby tracker for Pakistani families (feeds, sleep, diapers, vaccines, growth, in English and Urdu). Before it goes on the Play Store, Google needs a few people to test it for 2 weeks. Could you help?

1. On your Android phone, open this link while signed in to your Gmail: <LINK>
2. Tap **Become a tester**, then **Download it on Google Play** and install Palna.
3. Sign up and use it for a few days (a pretend baby is fine!).
4. Please **keep the app installed for at least 14 days**. That's the part Google counts.

Anything confusing or broken? Just reply here or email hello@palnacare.com. Thank you! 🙏

# Invite message for iPhone testers

Send once Apple has approved the build for external testing (TestFlight >
Ext-Test). Public link (open to anyone, limit 100):
https://testflight.apple.com/join/TNv2mUxa
iPhone only, iOS 15 or newer. There's no 14-day rule here; testers can
leave any time.

---

## English

Assalam o alaikum! I've made **Palna**, a baby tracker for Pakistani families (feeds, sleep, diapers, vaccines, growth, in English and Urdu). It isn't on the App Store yet. Could you test it on your iPhone?

1. Install **TestFlight** from the App Store (Apple's free app for testing new apps).
2. On your iPhone, open this link: https://testflight.apple.com/join/TNv2mUxa
3. Tap **Accept**, then **Install**.
4. Sign up and use it for a few days (a pretend baby is fine!).

Anything confusing or broken? Take a screenshot and TestFlight will offer to send it to me, or reply here or email hello@palnacare.com. Thank you! 🙏
